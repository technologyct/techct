Acting quickly matters

Do these three things now

If an intruder is active, the sooner you act the better. These steps contain the damage and protect the evidence that makes a permanent fix possible.

I

Disconnect the device

Turn off Wi-Fi or unplug the network cable on the affected computer to cut off active remote access. Don't power it off—just take it offline.

II

Don't wipe or reset anything

Reinstalling Windows or factory-resetting destroys the evidence of how they got in—and can destroy your data. Without that answer, they get back in. Leave everything exactly as it is.

III

Call me ASAP

The sooner you reach a professional, the less an intruder can do. Call me and we'll secure your accounts and devices together—starting with the ones that matter most.
